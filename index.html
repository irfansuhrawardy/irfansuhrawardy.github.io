About

Irfan ul Aslam Suhrawardy is a poet and author whose work explores spirituality, reflection, impermanence, silence, and the deeper realities hidden beneath everyday experience. Through poetry and prose, he seeks to illuminate the unseen dimensions of life and invite readers into a more contemplative engagement with the world.
